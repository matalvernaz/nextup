What the file should look like

A heading row, and a column this recognises the name of. These all work, whatever order the columns are in and whether they are separated by commas, semicolons or tabs:

  • Music: Artist, Album or Artist Name(s), Track Name — which is what a Spotify playlist export gives you.
  • Films and series: Title, Year, or Name, Year — which is what a Letterboxd export gives you.
  • Books: Title, Author.

A list with no headings at all works too, as long as it is one thing per line and nothing else. For music, write it Artist - Title and the two are read apart.

Up to {{ max_rows }} rows at a time. Anything the catalogue has no record of is reported rather than dropped, so a row that goes missing is one you can see.
