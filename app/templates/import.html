What the file should look like

Put column headings in the first row. The columns can be in any order, separated by commas, semicolons or tabs. These headings work:

  • Music: Artist and Album, or Artist and Track. A Spotify playlist export works as it is.
  • Films and series: Title and Year. A Letterboxd export works as it is.
  • Books: Title and Author.

A list with no headings works too, with one title on each line. For music, write each line as Artist - Title.
